# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Prospective and returning dental patients in the clinic's local area, browsing
mostly on phones. They arrive anxious or skeptical (dental visits carry real
fear for many people) and they are evaluating trust before they evaluate price.
The job to be done: feel confident enough about R Dental Centre's competence,
gentleness, and modernity to book a call.

## Product Purpose

A marketing site for R Dental Centre, a modern dental practice positioning
itself on "advanced technology with a gentle touch." The site exists to convert
a visitor's nervousness into a booked appointment by demonstrating clinical
expertise, a calm environment, and real patient outcomes. Success = the visitor
books a call or leaves trusting the practice.

## Brand Personality

Calm, clinical, reassuring. Three words: precise, gentle, modern. The voice is
plain and confident, never salesy or fear-mongering. It speaks the way a good
dentist does: clear about what will happen, unhurried, warm without being
chummy. Emotional goal: lower the visitor's shoulders.

## Anti-references

- Generic "AI healthcare SaaS" landing pages: cyan gradients, abstract blob
  illustrations, identical icon-card grids.
- Loud discount-dentistry sites: starbursts, "50% OFF", clip-art teeth.
- Cold corporate hospital sites: stocky, gray, impersonal.
- Editorial-magazine affectation (display-serif italic drop caps, broadsheet
  grids) that would read as a fashion title, not a clinic.

## Design Principles

1. **Lower the shoulders.** Every choice should reduce anxiety: generous space,
   soft motion, calm color, human faces. Nothing jarring.
2. **Show the room, not a metaphor.** Real photography of the practice, the
   team, and the technology beats abstract illustration. Trust is concrete.
3. **Expert confidence, quietly.** State capability plainly; let credentials,
   numbers, and outcomes carry weight instead of adjectives.
4. **Premium through restraint.** Polish lives in spacing, type, and motion
   detail, not in decoration. One disciplined palette, used with discipline.
5. **Fast and findable.** Statically rendered, semantic, accessible; the site
   must load instantly and be legible to search engines and screen readers.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA. Body text ≥4.5:1 contrast; large text ≥3:1. Full keyboard
navigation and visible focus states. All motion has a
`prefers-reduced-motion` fallback (crossfade or instant). Images carry
descriptive alt text. Anxious and older users are core, so legibility and
restraint outrank visual ambition wherever they conflict.
